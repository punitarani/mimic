# M13: E3b, M12's selection against v4's (ADR-0045)

E3b asks whether M12's breadth costs or saves questions. Only real people can answer that (R6), and none are reachable
from this environment. What this milestone ships is the experiment, ready to start, and a readout that shows real
people only, with intervals. Everything below is scripted and checks the machinery. None of it is a result.

## The arms

| Arm | Config | Hash | Differs by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `control` | `cfg.e3b.control` | `834484a3…` | v8 without ADR-0044's balance, cap, floor, group deadline, category targets and top-up; sweep off |
| `v8` | `cfg.default.v8` | `08956a22…` | — |

Everything else is shared: the calibrated primary (so fidelity is computed the same way), ontology v2, reserve.v2,
gen.v3, gates.v3, reflect.v2, the trust ramp, the consent confirmation and "Prefer not to say". People are allocated
1:1 by `hash(mimicId)`, and new mimics only.

## Offline cohort by arm (scripted, not a result)

```
pnpm eval -- cohort --preset e3b --people 8 --turns 32 --db data/e3b-offline.sqlite --blobs data/e3b-offline-blobs
pnpm eval -- rubric --data data/e3b-offline.sqlite --arm
pnpm eval -- arms --data data/e3b-offline.sqlite --population all
```

Eight personas, each run once in every arm, so the arms see the same people. They vary occupation, categories
(three with one category off) and consents (from none to all five). Offline fakes generate and predict; the scripted
answerer is deterministic per prompt.

| Arm | Every category within 15–40% by 30 | Facet groups touched by 20 | Consented sensitive facets by 30 | Sensitive in the first 5 | Generated questions passing `concrete` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| control | 0 of 5 people with all four categories | 58 of 73 (no person touched every group) | 32 of 44 (5 of 7 people reached all) | 0 | 158 of 158 |
| v8 | 5 of 5 | 73 of 73 (8 of 8) | 44 of 44 (7 of 7) | 0 | 137 of 137 |

Mean shares by 30 with four categories:
- **Control:** psychology 51% (39–61%), values 13%, life 30%, work 13% (4–25%).
- **v8:** psychology 34% (32–36%), values 24%, life 27%, work 24%.

The trust ramp holds in both arms. No sensitive question came before answer 7 for anyone, which the test suite
checks (`packages/eval/test/e3b.test.ts`).

The arms readout on the same cohort, run with `--population all`, which labels its output "a check of the machinery,
not a result":

| Arm | People | Fidelity at 20 [95% CI] | Reached 0.75 and stayed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| control | 8 | 0.58 [0.52, 0.66] | 0 of 8 |
| v8 | 8 | 0.64 [0.57, 0.71] | 0 of 8 |

v8 − control: +0.057 [−0.048, +0.153], not significant. With offline fakes the "fidelity" is how often arbitrary
predictions match a hash-chosen answer, so this row shows only that the readout runs and says "not significant" when
it should.

## Live evidence (scripted answerer, live generator, gates and Jev)

No new money was spent on a live scripted pair, because a scripted answerer can't measure efficiency. The existing
runs already cover both selections:
- **v4's selection:** M10's live sessions under the M10 candidate reached none of the consented sensitive facets in
  22 and 30 turns (`docs/reports/m10-concreteness.md`).
- **v8's selection:** M12's runs b and c reached 20 of 20 and touched 28 of 28 groups by question 20
  (`docs/reports/m12-rubric.md`).

## Running it on real people

1. In `/lab`, under Configs and experiments → Presets, press "Set up as a draft" for E3b. This registers both configs
   and saves the experiment as a draft. It starts nothing.
2. Review the draft (arms `control` and `v8`, 1:1), then press Start. Starting stops any other active experiment. New
   mimics are allocated from then on; existing ones keep their config.
3. Plan for about 64 real, research-consented people per arm. That assumes an SD of about 6 questions to sustain, or
   0.10 in fidelity at 20, to detect a 3-question or a 0.05 difference (α 0.05, power 0.8). Check both SDs at about 20
   people per arm, and report at the planned size, not on a peek.
4. Read it:

   ```
   pnpm eval -- export --env prod --out data/e3b.sqlite
   pnpm eval -- arms --data data/e3b.sqlite
   pnpm eval -- rubric --data data/e3b.sqlite --arm --population real
   ```

   `arms` counts real people only by default. Its primary metric is fidelity at 20, and its secondary metric is the
   questions to sustain 0.75 with the share who get there. Each arm gets a 95% bootstrap interval, and v8 − control
   gets its own. `/lab` shows the per-arm curves for real people only.

The browser check of the preset is `scripts/browser/lab-preset.mjs` (screenshot `docs/screenshots/m13-lab-preset.png`).

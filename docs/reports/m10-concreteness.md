# M10: concreteness of served questions under the candidate config (ADR-0042)

Scripted sessions, not research results. A scripted user answered with a fixed per-prompt policy (`policy: consistent`),
so nothing here says anything about prediction accuracy or about real people. What it measures is the questions the
pipeline served: live gen.v3 (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash), live gates.v3 (Jev) and the live `voi` selector, under
`--config m10-candidate` (the default config on ontology v2 with reserve.v2, gen.v3, gates.v3 and reflect.v2).

```
pnpm eval -- session --live --config m10-candidate --turns 30 --script packages/eval/scripts/m10/<script>.json
```

| Session | Script | Turns | Spend |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| nurse | Nurse, every sensitive area consented | 22 of 30 turns (stopped by a 30-minute timeout on the command) | $0.108 |
| accountant | Accountant, no sensitive consent | 30 turns | $0.151 |
| teacher | Teacher, politics and health consented | 30 turns | $0.16 |

The sessions ran before M10 was stacked on #18, so their candidate carried the shadow predictors of that time rather
than `cfg.default.v6`'s; shadows play no part in which questions are generated, gated or selected.

The first ten questions are the `anchors.v1` items (IPIP self-ratings and the fixed economic games), shared with every
config and not generated, so R1 counts the adaptive questions only. Repeat probes are also left out.

## Result

- **Concrete by hand: 46 of 48 served adaptive questions (96%).** The rubric asks for at least 80%.
- **Concrete by the gate:** 48 of 48 passed `concrete` at p ≥ 0.4 when they were pooled (every served question passed every gate).
- **Source:** 48 of 48 were generated by gen.v3; the reserve bank was never needed.
- **Workplace scenes:** 13 of 48 (27%); 3 of them measured a facet outside "Work and money", against gen.v3's instruction. Everyone here selected every category, so this is guidance, not the hard rule for people without "Work and money" (which the tests cover).
- **Sensitive questions:** 0. The two scripts with consents reached no sensitive facet in 22 and 30 turns: the belief-driven targets never ranked one first. Reaching every consented sensitive facet by question 30 is the sweep in M12 (ADR-0044), measured there (R4).
- **Categories of the measured facet:** psychology 19, values 11, life 8, work 10. Category balance is M12's (R2).

The labeller is the implementer, labelling from the question text with the gate column hidden, using the definitions in
`packages/eval/labeled/gates.v3.json`. The two questions labelled not concrete are near misses: a work-preference question
and a Saturday described by habits, the same patterns labelled not concrete in the calibration set. The gate passed both
(p ≥ 0.4), which matches its one miss in calibration.
